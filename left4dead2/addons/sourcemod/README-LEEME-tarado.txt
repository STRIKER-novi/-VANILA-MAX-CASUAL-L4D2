Go to the "CONFIGS" folder.

Open and edit the "admins.cfg" and "admins_simple.ini" files.

You can use any text editor, such as Notepad.

Fill in the files with the required information, such as your Steam ID, or assign yourself a rank.

And that's it! Now you're ready to play.
